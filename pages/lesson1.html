LESSON 01

Introduction to Business Studies

Learn the basic introduction and foundation of Business Studies.

What is Business?

A business is an activity carried out to provide goods or services to satisfy human needs and wants.

Importance of Business

Businesses play an important role in an economy by providing goods and services, creating employment opportunities and generating income.

Main Areas of Business

  • Production of goods and services
  • Marketing and selling
  • Financial activities
  • Human resource management

Learning Summary

In this lesson, you learned the basic idea of business, its importance and some of the main areas involved in business activities.
